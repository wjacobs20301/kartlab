# Native Kart Lab for TOTK 1.1.0

This development build targets TOTK **1.1.0** (build D5AD6AC71EF53E3E52417C1B81DBC9B4142AA3B3). It uses the locally extracted Mario standard kart and native TOTK riding. Tested in Ryujinx 1.3.3; modded Switch execution still needs hardware validation.

Load a mainland save, stand on open ground and briefly press **ZL + ZR + D-pad Up**, then release all three (PS4: **L2 + R2 + Up**). This spawns a kart, or recalls your existing unoccupied kart. Approach the kart's rear until **A: Control** appears and press A to get in.

Driving uses **Mario Kart 8 Deluxe controls**: **A** gas (let go of A once after getting in), **B** brake (keep holding when stopped to reverse), **left stick** steer, **R or ZR** hop and drift (hold while steering, release for a mini-turbo: blue, orange, then purple), **X** get out. The right stick still moves the camera. While you are in the kart the controller hides A, B, R, ZR and X from the game, adding about one frame (33 ms) of input delay; on foot every button behaves normally.

The normal startup controller waits for your input. It does not load a save, automatically spawn a kart, move Link into position, or run a timed test. It compiles once at startup and keeps one yielding module alive. No network listener starts. A tracked kart remains available after dismounting. The same shortcut recalls it without creating a duplicate; dismount before recalling. A world load clears the temporary reference. Actor lifetime tracking still uses guarded temporary references and needs broader stress testing.

**New in this build: animated wheels.** The tires roll with the kart's measured travel (forward and reverse) and the front wheels steer with the left stick, easing to about 26 degrees at full lock, including while stationary. The steering wheel in the cockpit turns with the stick too, and Link's hands follow it. The controller drives the Cart submodel's wheel bones each frame through a validated memory path; if anything about that path does not match, it skips the animation for that frame rather than writing. See `research/native-wheel-animation.md`.

Handling is built from Mario Kart 8 Deluxe data for Mario on the Standard Kart: acceleration, top speed (about 14 m/s), steering, drift turning, mini-turbo charge tiers and boosts come from its Performance tables and a recovered acceleration step. Braking, coasting, reverse, grip mapping and the world scale are tuned for TOTK. It is not the complete Mario Kart physics engine; see `research/mk-handling.md`. Native gravity and collision remain active, and a stopped kart holds still on slopes.

This experimental asset arrangement changes Control Stick actors to use the kart submodel and Cart contact hulls. It also replaces the native Cart visual, shared textures and vehicle idle animations. Other uses of these actors/resources are affected. Use the isolated development mod; a separate custom actor remains future work. A native TOTK seated lower-body pose is combined with the vehicle upper-body animation. Seat and hand fit are still being refined, and the entry/exit transitions use native TOTK actions. Independent suspension, items, off-road and air handling, and Mario characters are not implemented yet.

The runtime includes fixes for uninitialized spawn flags and an incorrect upstream TOTK1.1.0 linear-velocity address, plus an additive angular-velocity binding, reads startup scripts of up to 65,535 bytes (the original limit was 8,191), and keeps the player's raw controller input readable while the kart remaps controls. Exact pinned source, GPL license, source patches and byte-identical reproduction tools are included in the package. The original games and extracted inputs remain untouched.

For Ryujinx, install the package's `mod/exefs` and `mod/romfs` together under a single TOTK mod directory and copy `switch-sdcard` contents into its SD-card directory. Restart the game after changing executable/assets. For Atmosphere, the intended layout is `atmosphere/contents/0100F2C0115B6000/{exefs,romfs}` plus `totk_lotuskit` at the SD root; hardware compatibility is unverified. Disable this isolated mod and remove its startup script to restore ordinary actors and controls. Keep the provided backups of any preceding mod and save.
